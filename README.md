# 🌐 IdentityOS

<h3 align="center">
The Operating System for Your Digital Identity
</h3>

<p align="center">
Transform resumes, portfolios and developer profiles into an immersive operating-system style identity experience.
</p>

---

## ✨ Imagine if your resume booted like an operating system...

Most resume analyzers generate another boring report.

**IdentityOS does something different.**

It boots.

It scans your digital footprint.

It builds your developer identity.

It detects your technology stack.

It estimates your developer archetype.

It generates an interactive identity dashboard.

And finally...

It lets you explore your identity through an interactive terminal.

# 🌟 Why IdentityOS?

IdentityOS is not designed to replace a resume.

It is designed to **reimagine** how developers interact with their digital identity.

Instead of another static report, IdentityOS creates an immersive operating-system experience that transforms developer profiles into an interactive identity dashboard.
